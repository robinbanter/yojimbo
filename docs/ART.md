# Yojimbo art direction
- **Style:** bold sumi-e ink brushwork on warm washi paper, flat vermilion accents, generous negative space, woodblock-print restraint, hand-stamped seal shapes without characters.
- **Palette:** washi cream #f1ebdd, ink black #141414, vermilion #d8432f, muted indigo night #2b3550, pale gold #c9a961.
- **Subjects:** a ronin bodyguard keeping night watch beside a sleeping figure; a paper lantern guarding a small glowing chart; a shield-shaped crescent moon; a katana laid across a ledger; a guardian silhouette in rain.
- **Banned:** real brands or logos, readable text or kanji, real people, blood or violence, other designers' artwork.
